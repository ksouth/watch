# Provenance and integrity

Each captured resource should retain its source URL and final URL, capture time,
content type, byte count, SHA-256 hash, retrieval status, robots decision,
discovered links, and change history.

Missing, blocked, failed, and pending resources are states to report explicitly.
They are not permission to delete an earlier archive.

Generated indexes and summaries are derived artifacts. Keep them distinguishable
from source captures and human-authored interpretation.
