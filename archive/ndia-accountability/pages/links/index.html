Other projects

Links to other projects aiming to raise awareness, help participants, or correct harms caused by the NDIA

  • Harm Tracker Project

    A national tool from the Australian Neurodivergent Parents Association (ANPA), in partnership with the Nobody Worse Off Coalition, for reporting how NDIS cuts are hurting people.

  • NDIS Decoded

    Independent, participant-authored plain-language explainers of NDIS terminology, policy changes and participant rights, including monthly summaries of Administrative Review Tribunal decisions.

  • NDIS Appeals Database Project

    A searchable database of NDIS appeal decisions from the AAT and the Administrative Review Tribunal, compiled by the University of Queensland Pro Bono Centre.

  • Bloc the Cuts

    A grassroots coalition of disabled people, families, support workers and businesses campaigning against the proposed NDIS cuts through an open letter to the Senate.

  • Elly Desmarchelier

    Writing from disability advocate Elly Desmarchelier on the NDIS, disability policy and rights, including her firsthand account of working inside the NDIA.

  • NDIS Insights with Dr George

    An independently produced NDIS podcast from Dr George Taleporos, combining lived experience, policy analysis and interviews to explain reforms, rights and decisions affecting participants.

  • Common Ground Disability

    Lived-experience-informed writing that translates NDIS legislation, policy and reform into practical explanations and tools for participants and families.

  • Every Australian Counts

    The grassroots campaign that helped win the introduction of the NDIS and continues to organise people with disability, families and supporters to protect and improve the Scheme.

  • Observing Practice Quality Tool

    A free research-based tool and app from La Trobe University for observing and assessing the quality of frontline support received by people with intellectual disability.

  • Mable / NDIS Budget Tracker

    A community-built Google Sheets tool that turns Mable support-worker exports into an NDIS budget tracker with categorised transactions, summaries and import history.
